Travel and Expense Policy

Employees traveling for business must book flights and hotels through the approved travel portal whenever possible. Any booking made outside the portal requires prior written approval from a manager. Economy class is the default for flights under six hours; longer flights may be booked in premium economy with manager sign-off.

Meal expenses while traveling are reimbursed up to a daily cap that varies by city, published in the travel portal's rates table. Alcohol is not reimbursable under any circumstance. Receipts are required for any single expense over twenty five dollars, and all expense reports must be submitted within thirty days of the trip's end date.

Reimbursements are processed within two payroll cycles after an expense report is approved by the employee's manager and the finance team. Expense reports missing receipts or exceeding policy limits are sent back to the employee for correction rather than being rejected outright.

Personal expenses, even if incurred during a business trip, are never reimbursable and must be clearly separated from business expenses on the report.
